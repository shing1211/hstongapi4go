9. Known limitations / caveats
================================================================================
- `make docs-check` itself could not be executed (`make` absent, `python3`
  stubbed); its three underlying commands were each run directly and exit 0:
  check_links.py, check_i18n.py, mkdocs build --strict.
- Native-content guard thresholds (see scripts/check_i18n.py NATIVE_CONTENT) are
  deliberately far below what a real translation produces, so they catch an
  untranslated English copy, not translation quality.
- Heading tolerance is 2 (documented in the script docstring and TRANSLATING.md);
  all six files currently have exactly 13 headings (zero drift).
- The Korean translation of the vendor name uses the Hanja form 華盛; the common
  Latin form "HStong" is kept alongside it.
